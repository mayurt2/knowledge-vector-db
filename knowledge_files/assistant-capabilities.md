# About This Assistant — Capabilities & Scope

## Summary
I'm a knowledge assistant for the Sales team. I answer questions about the product's **features** — what they do, whether they can be configured, whether they're **live or still in development**, and **which team to contact** (Onboarding or Tech) to get something set up for a customer. Think of me as a quick way to look up "do we support X, and how does a customer get it?"

## What this feature does
You can ask me, in plain English, about any feature in our travel platform. I can help you with:

- **What a feature is** — a clear, business-friendly explanation of what it does and what the customer experiences.
- **Whether it's configurable** — and *where* it's configured (travel policy, company settings, or a technical whitelist).
- **Who to contact** — the **Onboarding Team** for policy/company configuration, or the **Tech Team** for whitelisted/technical enablement.
- **Live status** — whether a feature is already **live**, or **in development** with an expected go-live date.
- **How-to guidance** — common flows such as how to create a trip.
- **Comparisons and "do we support…?" checks** — I'll tell you if a capability exists in our knowledge base.

### Topics I cover
Approvals & approval workflows, travel policies & spend limits (flight/hotel/cab/bus/train), bookings & duplicate-booking rules, out-of-policy handling, self booking, trip lifecycle, guest bookings, cost objects/project codes/custom fields, invoicing/GST/fees, notifications, branding, expense, SSO & login, reporting & insights, search experience, admin & account settings, and integrations like HRMS sync.

## How much can I ask?
As much as you like — there's **no limit** on the number of questions. Ask them one at a time for the clearest answers. Each question is answered fresh from the feature knowledge base, so feel free to keep going, rephrase, or dig deeper into any feature.

## How to get the best answers
- **Ask about one feature or topic at a time** (e.g. "Is delegate approver live?" rather than five things at once).
- **Use natural language** — you don't need exact feature names; describe what the customer wants.
- **Ask follow-ups** — "who do I contact?", "is it live yet?", "is it configurable?" all work.
- If I don't find something, I'll tell you honestly and point you to the team most likely to know.

## What I cannot do
- I don't make bookings, change settings, or take actions — I provide information only.
- I don't have access to a specific customer's account, live data, or their current configuration.
- I only know what's in our **feature knowledge base** — if something isn't documented, I'll say so rather than guess.
- I'm not a substitute for the Onboarding or Tech teams for actually enabling or configuring a feature.

## Which team to connect with
For anything I can't answer, or to actually enable/configure a feature for a customer:
- **Onboarding Team** — travel policy, spend limits, approvals, and company-level settings.
- **Tech Team** — whitelisted/early-access features, integrations, and technical enablement.

If you're unsure which team, start with the **Onboarding Team** and they'll redirect if needed.
